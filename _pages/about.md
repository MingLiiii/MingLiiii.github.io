I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Maryland**, advised by [Prof. Tianyi Zhou](https://tianyizhou.github.io/). I study how to train LLMs, understand their reasoning and behavior, and rigorously evaluate the capabilities and reliability of models and agentic systems.

**[2026 Apple Scholar in AI/ML](https://machinelearning.apple.com/updates/apple-scholars-aiml-2026)** · **2× ACL Oral** · **ACL’26 SAC Highlight**

My research experience includes internships at Amazon, Microsoft Research, and Adobe, and a visiting research appointment at MBZUAI. I also serve as an *ACL ARR Area Chair* and reviewer for major conferences.

### Research

(1) **Data-Centric LLM Post-Training:** I develop methods to select and reuse high-value training signals, from instruction-data selection ([IFD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12032), [Superfiltering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00530)) to critical-step agent tuning ([ATLaS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02197)). I also use [layer-wise gradient analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10766) to understand how instruction and reasoning data shape post-training and what makes training data effective.

(2) **Reasoning & Model Behavior:** I study how training and inference shape reasoning structure and reliability, using functional analysis of reasoning traces ([ThinkARM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19995), **ACL’26 Oral & SAC Highlight**) and layer-wise gradient analysis ([Fast vs. Slow Thinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23743), **ACL’25 Oral**). I also investigate reasoning failures such as [overthinking under missing premises](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06514) and [what RL improves in visual reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12395).

(3) **AI Evaluation & Reliability:** I design controlled evaluations to probe capability boundaries and unreliable judgments, including collective behavior in AI agent societies ([Moltbook](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14299); [Science News interview](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/ai-agent-teams-fail-succeed-bots-chaos)) and [rhetorical sensitivity in AI reviewers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08975). Related directions include multimodal capabilities ([ColorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10514)), efficient evaluation ([BenTo](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ki7b0qD11r)), and agent evaluation environments ([ClawEnvKit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18543)).

I also explore **Human-AI Cognitive Alignment**, especially how model reasoning and difficulty judgments relate to human cognition: [Difficulty Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18880), [Item Difficulty Modeling](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/00131644251344973), and [Episode2Difficulty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28186).

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and collaborations.
